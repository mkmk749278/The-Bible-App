Privacy Policy — Manna

App: com.manna.bible · Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Contact: mulakapati446@gmail.com

Manna is built privacy-first. We designed it so the app is fully useful without an account, without tracking, and without ads — ever.

The short version

  • No account is required to use any core feature.
  • We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
  • No analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs are in the app.
  • Your spiritual data stays on your device — your notes, highlights, bookmarks, prayers, reading position and settings are stored locally and are not uploaded to us or to any cloud service. They are also excluded from Android's automatic Google cloud backup by design. (On Android 12 and later, Android's own phone-to-phone transfer can copy them directly to your new phone when you set it up; nothing passes through us.)
  • Optional AI features send what you ask to Google's Gemini service — see below.

What data the app handles

Stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone:

  • Highlights, bookmarks, and notes
  • Prayer journal entries and reflections
  • Reading progress and your last reading position
  • App settings (languages, theme, audio preferences, reminders)
  • Downloaded Bible translations

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Network requests the app makes

Manna connects to the internet only for these purposes, and only to these hosts:

  • bible.helloao.org — the key-less, MIT-licensed Free Use Bible API, used to download Bible translations and to fetch streamed narrated-audio links. Only generic content references (translation id, book, chapter) are sent. No personal data is sent.
  • generativelanguage.googleapis.com (Google Gemini) — optional. Used only when you use one of the AI features below and a Gemini API key has been configured for the build (otherwise they are hidden or disabled and no request is made):
    • Explain this passage — the passage text and reference, your Bible language, and the church tradition you chose during setup (if any).
    • Crisis companion ("Tell me what's happening") — the words you type about your situation, your Bible language, whether it is night-time, and your tradition.
    • Verse cards ("find a verse for…") — the occasion you describe, your Bible language and your tradition.
    • Sermon builder — the passage reference, your tradition, language and the kind of congregation you chose.
    Manna does not send your name, account, contacts, location or any device identifier, and Manna does not store what you type. Requests identify the app (its package name and signing-certificate fingerprint) so the API key cannot be misused by other apps. Google processes these requests under the Gemini API terms. Because what you type in the crisis companion or verse cards may be personal, please avoid including names or other identifying details.

All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS. Offline-first features (reading bundled Bibles, on-device text-to-speech, notes, prayers, daily verse) work with no network at all.

On-device AI

On supported devices, "Explain this passage" can run on-device via Gemini Nano, in which case nothing is sent off the device. The other AI features always use the online service described above.

Stealth (Persecution) Mode

When Stealth Mode is on, your PIN is never stored — only a salted PBKDF2 hash. The app opens behind a calculator disguise, re-locks whenever you leave it, hides its content from the Recents screen and screenshots, blanks the home-screen widget and does not show reminder notifications.

Permissions

  • Internet / Network state — to download Bibles and stream narrated audio.
  • Notifications — to show the optional daily-verse reminder you choose.
  • Run after restart — to re-arm your daily reminder after the phone reboots.

We request no location, contacts, microphone, camera, or storage permissions.

Children's privacy

Manna does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads and no data collection. The optional AI features described above are processed by Google and are not directed at children.
